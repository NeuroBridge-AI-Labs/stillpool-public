<Simple>
A model reads your words. It cuts them into small parts called tokens. Then it guesses which part comes next. It picks one and adds it to the reply. It does this again and again until the reply is done.
</Simple>

<Deep>
Autoregressive transformer architectures factorise the joint probability of a token sequence into conditional distributions, estimated through multi-head self-attention over learned positional representations.
</Deep>
