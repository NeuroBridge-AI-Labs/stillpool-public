## Emanuel's take, in full

> Some things I'd rather keep at home. A model that runs on my own computer lets me. My words stay on my machine. Think of a diary and a postcard. A postcard passes through other hands, while a diary stays in your drawer.

> For private notes, I want the diary. Is local always better? No. It's often slower, and the answers can be weaker. My machine uses the power, and it has to be a capable one.

> So I choose by task. Ordinary questions can travel. Private ones stay home. **Collect only what's needed. Send only what you must.** Privacy is a form of **respect**.
