## Emanuel's take, in full

> I believe much fear comes from not knowing. So look at what this lesson showed you. A chatbot picks a likely next piece of text. Then another, and another.

> Does knowing that make the risks vanish? No. A likely sentence can still be false. People can still use these tools to deceive. I won't wave that away. But fear grows in a dark room. Knowing is the light switch.

> With the light on, you see what's really there. Some of it needs real care. Some of it is only furniture. **Name the harm. Then build on what's possible.** That's a steadier place to stand than fear.
