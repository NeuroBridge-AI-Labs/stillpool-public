## Emanuel's take, in full

> I hear two stories about AI. In one it's a monster coming for us. In the other it's only a fancy calculator. I think both miss the point. Here's the image I use: an amplifier.

> An amplifier makes louder whatever is fed into it. Feed it care and you get more care. Feed it carelessness and you get more of that.

> So I don't ask what AI will do to us. I ask what we'll do with it. **Technology should amplify people, never replace them.** A teacher is still the teacher. A friend is still the friend. The tool is new. **The responsibility is still ours.**

## Opinion boundary
