## Emanuel's take, in full

> When an unfair result appears, people say the system decided. I don't accept that. The tool doesn't decide. People do. Someone chose the data. Someone chose the labels. Someone chose the goal, and where to use it.

> I think of those choices as seeds. A seed is small and easy to miss. Later it's the whole field. **We harvest what we seed.** Plant a narrow picture of people and you harvest unfair results.

> So who should check the field? The people who planted it, and the people who use it. **No design choice is neutral.** That's hard news, and good news. A choice can be made again.
