## Emanuel's take, in full

> I call AI a mirror of ourselves. It was built from our words, our pictures and our choices. So it shows our patterns back to us. The fair ones and the unfair ones.

> But this is no ordinary mirror. Someone chose the glass. Someone chose the frame and the angle. People decide what it's for, and where it points.

> So when you see something ugly in it, whose is it? Ours. That's uncomfortable. It's also hopeful, because what people chose, people can change. This pillar is about looking properly. With curiosity, and without fear. A mirror only helps if we're **willing to look**. **What we see there is ours to change.**
