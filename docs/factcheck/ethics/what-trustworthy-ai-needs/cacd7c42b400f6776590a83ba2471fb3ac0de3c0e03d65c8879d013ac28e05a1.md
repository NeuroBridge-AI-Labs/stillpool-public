> This lesson gave you seven requirements from the EU's expert group. They're guidelines, and good ones. Here is the lens I look through. **I hold four of them closest.**

> Transparency: if you can't see why a system said something, that's a problem. Fairness: a tool should close gaps, not widen them. Privacy: collect what's needed and nothing extra.
