## Emanuel's take, in full

> What follows is hope. I can't promise it. Nobody knows how AI will change work. Some tasks are already changing, and that's frightening when the task is yours. I won't pretend otherwise.

> But I keep asking one question. What is work for? I don't believe it's only for staying alive. We are not here merely to survive. We are here to evolve. Picture a tool that carries the heavy load. Your hands are free.

> What you do with free hands is the real choice. I'd choose creativity, care and wisdom. And I'd give credit honestly, to people first. **Free hands are a gift.** What we build with them is up to us.
