## Emanuel's take, in full

> The atom didn't decide to become a bomb. People decided. I keep that close when I read about AI harm.

> It's tempting to blame the tool. It's also tempting to blame the person who got hurt. I think both are wrong turns. Who answers when a system harms someone? The people who built it, sold it, and chose to use it. Name them, and name their roles.

> I picture a chain, where each link is one person's choice. Harm travels along it. So does care. **A tool has no intentions. People have them.** That's where responsibility lives. It's also where repair begins.
