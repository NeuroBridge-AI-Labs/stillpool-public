## Emanuel's take, in full

> I think in structure. Headings, keywords, one idea in each box. So when I write a prompt, I build it the way I'd want to read it.

> What I want. Who it's for. What shape the answer should take. I call that a scaffold. A scaffold isn't the building. It holds the work steady while it goes up.

> Does structure promise a good answer? No. It gives a clear request, and a clear request is easier to check. That's my experience, not a law. **Structure is how I think.** Clear thinking shows up as **clear asking**.
