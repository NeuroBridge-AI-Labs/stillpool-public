## Emanuel's take, in full

> I say **"a skill is an SOP for an AI"**. SOP means standard operating procedure: written steps for doing a job the same way every time. Workplaces run on them.

> Good ones hold more than steps. They say what must never happen, and why. That part matters most to me. When do the rules go in? At the start.

> Ethics added at the end is paint on a cracked wall. It looks fine and it holds nothing. So when I write a skill, the limits go in first. What it may do. What it must not do. Who checks the result. **Ethics is the foundation.** The steps are built on top of it.
