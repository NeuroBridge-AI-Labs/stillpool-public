## Emanuel's take, in full

> I like these tools, and I still check them. That isn't distrust. It's respect for the truth. An answer from AI is a claim. A citation is a signpost. A signpost only helps if you walk to where it points.

> So I walk. I open the link and look for the sentence. Sometimes it's there. Sometimes the page says something else. Sometimes there's no page at all.

> Which claims do I check? The ones I'd act on, or pass on to someone else. Health, law and money go to a qualified person. **Don't accept it passively. Question it.** The checking is where I learn the most.
